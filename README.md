# Defining and Relating Concepts in Medical Terminology: A Case Study on the Gut-Brain Interplay (LREC 2026)

License: [CC-by-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
 
This repository presents the terminology work carried out to analyze the conceptual dimension of terminology concerning the gut-brain interplay and associated neurodegenerative and mental health-related conditions.  

## Repository Structure

The file ```terminology_resource.xlsx``` comprises the analyzed concepts and their intensional definitions, associative relations with their typology, and the mapping with the [HERO ontology](https://hereditary.dei.unipd.it/ontology/).

Sheet ```Intensional Defintions```contains all analyzed concepts (Column "\<Concept>") and their intensional definition (Column "Intensional Definition").

Sheet ```Associative Relations``` lists all associative relations identified between concepts (Column "Relation Marker") and their typology. For each relation, we provide the typology of the relation according to Nuoppoen 2022 (Column "Relation Types Mapped to Nuopponen's Concept Relations"). If the identified relation does not match the relations in the typology of Nuopponen, we defined our own typology (Column "Proposed Relations").

Sheet ```HERO-Concepts```contains the mapping between concepts identified at a terminological level and the [HERO ontology](https://hereditary.dei.unipd.it/ontology/). 

Sheet ```HERO-Relations```contains the mapping between relations identified at a terminological level and the [HERO ontology](https://hereditary.dei.unipd.it/ontology/).
